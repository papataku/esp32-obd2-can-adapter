## 2. Induced-response contract and evidence gap

Proposed bounded transaction (subject to a separately verified physical contract): **one** 29-bit physical request to a single, individually authorized candidate, Service01 `0C/0D` only, at most **two total CAN Single Frame responses** from that candidate to this request (either one combined 6-byte `41 0C A B 0D X` response or separate correct one-PID replies). Reserve four 8-byte frames: TX request 1 + RX maximum 2 + one safety allowance = **800 bits**. Do not send any ISO-TP Flow Control in discovery. First Frame, Consecutive Frame, unexpected extra response, duplicate PID/source, unsupported negative result, wrong reply ID/format or capacity overflow => transaction invalid, revoke TX, STOP; do not assemble a success.

**Evidence status: UNCONFIRMED.** The three cited documents count *decoded PID responses* inside historical functional BATCH commands, explicitly counting `FF/CF` as one completed response. They do **not** provide a per-request, physical-addressed RAW CAN capture proving a maximum of two Single Frames, a maximum of one physical ECU transmitting, or absence of late/duplicate replies. In particular, `019A` has observed multi-frame replies and is excluded from discovery. It is incorrect to infer "two SF guaranteed" from `010C/010D` support bits, completed-response counts or a selector array limited to eight items. The proposed cap remains a **contract requiring proof**, not an established fact or an instruction to reserve less.

PR-B must NOT enable unrestricted vehicle TX solely from this document. Per owner decision (2026-10-29), a production-core **serial HIL** substitutes for the independent-analyzer bench, and S1 is the first strictly bounded owner-authorized vehicle observation of the physical response ceiling. Neither HIL nor the historical functional DB guarantees a real ECU's maximum number of responses. The ECU-specific 2-SF bound remains **UNCONFIRMED until S1 has been separately reviewed by owner and PM**, and even then it is an observed bound for that test, not an absolute guarantee. If evidence is absent, mark `UNCONFIRMED` and STOP. The adapter cannot silence an externally over-responding ECU; count all received traffic (including late frames) and latch out further TX on a breach.

## 3. Admission and worst-case 29-bit / 500 kbit/s calculation

For one Classical CAN extended 8-byte data frame: bit-stuffed region is 118 bits (SOF through CRC sequence), maximum additional stuff bits `floor((118-1)/4)=29`; CRC delimiter + ACK + EOF + 3-bit intermission = 13 bits. Hence **160 bits/frame** is the theoretical per-frame upper bound, including worst-case bit stuffing and intermission. Retain the existing **200 bits/frame** conservative admission cost, with no reductions.

| Item | Maximum reserved frames | At 200 bits/frame | At 160 bits/frame |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| One physical discovery | 4 | 800 bits | 640 bits |
| Four discoveries in one admission sequence | 16 | 3,200 bits | 2,560 bits |
| First regular physical acquisition (separately admitted) | 12 | 2,400 bits | 1,920 bits |
| Conservative 100 ms rolling-window overlap envelope (two discovery reservations + regular acquisition) | 20 | **4,000 / 5,000 bits** | 3,200 bits |
| Conservative 1,000 ms rolling-window cumulative envelope (four discoveries + regular acquisition) | 28 | **5,600 / 25,000 bits** | 4,480 bits |

The **4,000 / 5,000** and **5,600 / 25,000** figures are theoretical/conservative admission envelopes, **not externally measured on-wire traffic**, not an exact retransmission count and not a budget exemption. Bound requests to at least **200 ms between starts**, individual response window <=120 ms, strictly sequential completion/drain before another query, and the existing reservation's duration carried across rolling windows. Enforce both existing admission controllers: upper CanOwner and CAN Monitor before actual TX, counting other admitted activity, actual candidate RX/FC/late traffic, prior reservations and failed TX conservatively. If the rolling remaining budget cannot admit the full next transaction, do not transmit; STOP/Invalid as specified, not a partial charge. There is no functional discovery escape hatch. The previously rejected broadcast reservation is `(1+256*2)*200=102,600 bits` and remains prohibited.

## 5. First three seconds and staged vehicle handoff (future gates only)

**Before any vehicle ARM:** PR-B/PR-C implementation + security review, matched Ring freshness/epoch behavior, green host and virtual-ECU tests, M5Dial serial-HIL pass, verified CAN TX disabled in the HIL build, reviewed 200-bit theoretical budget and counters, approved candidate `{18DAF101}`, safe backup/rollback and explicit S1 owner authorization. **No external CAN analyzer is required by the 2026-10-29 owner decision.** This is an acceptance-policy change, not independent physical proof.

- **S1 — one physical request, no ongoing LIVE acquisition:** owner parks/P/brakes and logs baseline all-RX and TWAI error counters; explicit new ARM, at most one individually admitted physical `0C/0D` query, no discovery FC, then continue receiving/counting for 1,000 ms and **automatically STOP**. Log 100 ms/1 s conservative rolling estimates and all unexpected traffic; speed remains Invalid pending inspection. The response and stop/drain cycle is bounded by the 3,000 ms initial-acquisition safeguard; if timing or counters are uncertain, STOP. Owner and PM inspect the sanitized trace and approve/reject proceeding to S2. **S1 does not declare a full 3-second Fresh acquisition or any physical correctness proof.**
- **S2 — stationary 30 seconds at 5 Hz:** separate owner ARM => *new* epoch, not a continuation of the S1 pin. Fresh RAW-backed physical pin under the same candidate and admission gates, first genuine speed zero and matching source/age within 3 seconds or STOP; continue **at most 30 seconds** with immediate invalidation on anomaly. Owner checks genuine speed/RPM against stock displays, where those indications actually exist. After the observation STOP and get owner+PM review. Do not use a prior S1 pin to start S2.
- **S3 — driving:** fresh explicit owner approval, new ARM/source epoch and same 3-second fail-closed acquisition gate **while stationary before movement**. Only then may the approved controlled driving stage start; require continuously Fresh vehicle speed, same verified ECU, safe loss-of-data handling and visual stock speed/RPM comparison where possible. STOP cannot retract frames already transmitted, but must revoke new requests/FC. Do not self-authorize S3 based on S2 logs.

On all stages, auto-STOP at an unexpected third response, FF/CF, any discovery FC, other responder, 11-bit reply, missing/overflowed RX frame count, late reply, TWAI bus/error/overrun/drop, denied budget, stale sample, ECU ambiguity or fail-safe timeout. No automatic functional discovery or restart. If the strict two-SF physical response contract is not *observed* in S1, **S2/S3 remain blocked**.
